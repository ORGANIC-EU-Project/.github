## About ORGANIC

**ORGANIC** is a Horizon Europe Research and Innovation Action (GA 101178127) aiming to advance the biological transformation of Additive Manufacturing (AM) by integrating bio-inspired design, bio-intelligent technologies and sustainable materials.

The project combines advanced **Fused Granulate Fabrication (FGF)** technologies, generative design and artificial intelligence to develop adaptive and resource-efficient manufacturing systems capable of continuously improving their performance.

Inspired by biological systems, ORGANIC introduces the concept of **Gentelligence**, enabling manufacturing processes to capture, reuse and evolve knowledge across successive production cycles.

The developed technologies will be validated in the wind energy sector, targeting the sustainable production of lightweight, high-performance, bio-inspired components.

## Our Open-Source Developments

This GitHub organisation serves as the central public repository space for software, algorithms and technical resources developed by the ORGANIC consortium.

### 1. [Advanced CAx Tool](https://github.com/ORGANIC-EU-Project/Advanced-CAx-Tool)

An integrated computer-aided design and optimisation framework for generating bio-inspired structures and exploring process–structure–property–performance (PSPP) relationships.

**Key areas:** Generative design · Topology optimisation · Multi-objective optimisation · Sustainability by design

### 2. [Advanced AM Fabrication System](https://github.com/ORGANIC-EU-Project/Advanced-AM-Fabrication-System)

Software developments supporting intelligent FGF additive manufacturing through advanced sensing, monitoring, adaptive toolpath planning and real-time self-X control.

**Key areas:** FGF manufacturing · Process monitoring · Adaptive control · First-Time-Right manufacturing

### 3. [Gentelligence Digital Framework](https://github.com/ORGANIC-EU-Project/Gentelligence-digital-framework)

A bio-intelligent digital framework enabling the long-term evolution of additive manufacturing through knowledge modelling, digital twins and multi-objective reinforcement learning.

**Key areas:** Digital twins · Knowledge modelling · Reinforcement learning · Continuous process optimisation
